Doctor's Slip Decoded

How to Read Your Optical Prescription

Understand the acronyms and numbers written by your eye doctor.

Sample Prescription
Eye SPHSphere CYLCylinder AXISAxis ADDReading PDPupil Dist.
ODRight -2.25 -0.75 180° +1.50 31.5
OSLeft -2.00 -0.50 175° +1.50 31.5
OD vs. OS

OD: Oculus Dexter (Right Eye).
OS: Oculus Sinister (Left Eye).

SPH (Sphere)

Indicates lens power for distance or reading. Negative (-) means nearsighted, positive (+).

CYL & AXIS

Corrects astigmatism (irregular cornea curve). Axis (1° to 180°) determines orientation.

PD (Pupillary Distance)

The distance between your pupils in millimeters. Ensures the optical center aligns.

Longevity & Care

How to Care for Your Precision Lenses

Simple daily habits to protect your multi-coatings for years to come.

DO: Use Lukewarm Water

Rinse lenses under gentle lukewarm tap water before wiping to wash away micro-grit that causes scratches.

DON'T: Use Shirt Corners

Wood-fiber t-shirts and paper napkins contain abrasive micro-fibers that wear away anti-reflective coats.

DON'T: Leave in Hot Cars

Extreme dashboard heat can cause the multi-layer AR coating and lens substrate to expand at different rates.
