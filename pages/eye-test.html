What to Expect

Your Clinical Exam Experience

A gentle, unhurried, non-invasive process engineered for diagnostic accuracy.

01

Digital Wavefront Pre-Test

Before meeting the doctor, our automated corneal topographer measures higher-order optical aberrations and baseline refractive power in 90 seconds.

02

Doctor Consultation

Dr. Vance conducts a subjective lens refraction, tests ocular binocular coordination, and evaluates accommodation power for digital screen.

03

Personalized Lens Prescription

Receive your verified optical prescription immediately. Our frame stylists will assist in measuring your exact Pupillary Distance (PD) and pantoscopic tilt.
