Our Heritage & Ethos

Crafting Vision. Protecting Sight.

Born in 2021, Veyra was founded on a simple observation: eyewear should never force a compromise between architectural beauty and medical precision.

Our Story

From a Single Atelier Workbench to Modern Ophthalmic Centers

In 2021, Master Optician Marcus Sterling and Dr. Elena Vance partnered to challenge the traditional optical industry. For decades, consumers had two stark choices: cheap, mass-market frames with low-grade lenses, or astronomically marked-up designer licensing names.

Veyra eliminated unnecessary intermediaries. We source pure bio-acetate directly from Mazzucchelli 1849 in Italy and Japanese Beta-Titanium from Sabae, pairing them with an in-house digital lens laboratory and board-certified eye examinations under one roof.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, world-class vision correction wrapped in enduring, minimalist.

Our Vision

To make high-acuity preventative eye health accessible, beautiful, and completely stress.

Materials & Integrity

Engineered for a Lifetime of Wear

We scrutinize every millimeter, from surgical titanium screws to cotton-derived bio-resins.

Mazzucchelli 1849 Bio-Acetate

Crafted in Castiglione Olona, Italy using renewable wood cellulose and plant-derived plasticizers. Biodegradable, hypoallergenic, and warm to the touch.

Sabae Japanese Beta-Titanium

Sourced from Fukui Prefecture, Japan. Ultra-flexible with high tensile memory, weighing less than 15 grams while resisting perspiration and saltwater corrosion.

Free-Form Digital Lenses

Diamond-machined point by point. Minimizes edge distortion in high prescriptions and delivers 99.6% light transmission with our broadband AR coat.
